A practical starting point

Your next step starts with the right question.

This short guide helps you organise what you know, what is missing and who to speak with next.

01 / 05

A clearer way forward

Separate the rules, the data and the design choices.

Packaging questions often combine different issues. Keep them distinct so you can find the right answer and involve the right people.

01 /

Understand your role

List products, packaging, suppliers and markets. Note who buys, fills, imports or places each pack on the market before drawing conclusions about responsibility.

02 /

Find the missing data

Ask suppliers for specifications, materials, component weights, documentation and intended use. Record open questions instead of guessing.

03 /

Decide what comes next

Use official sources for rule questions, national schemes for local obligations and a packaging partner for design, materials and prototypes.
