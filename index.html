    How it works

    Start with one pack.

    Pick one product from your shelf. Work through these three questions with purchasing, design and your supplier.

    Take the readiness test →
    What is it made of?

    Take the pack apart. Record the weight, material and function of each part, including labels, coatings and closures. Start with Article 6 when reviewing recyclability.

    What can you document?

    Request dated specifications for the packaging type. Identify the manufacturer, then check which documentation and EU declaration requirements apply under Articles 15, 38 and 39 and Annexes VII and VIII. The pack must be traceable to its documentation.

    Who owns the next step?

    Map who makes, imports, distributes or unpacks the packaging in each market. Use Article 3 and Chapter IV (Articles 15–23) for roles and duties. Check producer registration under Article 44 and the national rules, including the Danish Packaging Order for Denmark.

    Questions about PPWR

    What is PPWR?

    PPWR stands for Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation. Regulation (EU) 2025/40 sets rules for packaging placed on the EU market and covers topics such as packaging design, recyclability and producer responsibility.

    Read Regulation (EU) 2025/40

    When do the new packaging rules apply?

    The PPWR has generally applied since 12 August 2026. Some provisions and specific requirements apply on other dates. Check the dates for the requirement and packaging in question.

    Read the European Commission implementation guidance

    What is the difference between PPWR and Danish producer responsibility?

    PPWR is the EU regulation on packaging and packaging waste. In Denmark, the responsible producer generally first makes the packaging available on the Danish market. Rules vary with packaging type and role, including certain cases of unpacking. Check who must register with Dansk Producentansvar and finance waste management.

    Read about Danish producer responsibility

    What does the film protect against?

    The film can protect the product from moisture or dirt, depending on the material and how it is sealed.

    Could a lighter insert protect the product just as well?

    The insert holds the product in place. Compare the current insert with a lighter one in a transport test.

    Could the carton be smaller and still do its job?

    Measure the product, insert and carton. Check how much space you need for protection and packing.

    By 1 January 2030, manufacturers or importers must ensure that packaging placed on the market is designed with the minimum weight and volume needed for it to function.
