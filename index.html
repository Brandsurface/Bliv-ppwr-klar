    How it works

    Start with one pack.

    Pick one product from your shelf. Work through these three questions with purchasing, design and your supplier.

    Take the readiness test →
    What is it made of?

    Take the pack apart. Record the weight, material and function of each part, including labels, coatings and closures. Start with Article 6 when reviewing recyclability.

    What can you document?

    Request current specifications and drawings. Record the date and supplier for each file. Article 15 and Annex VII describe the manufacturer’s technical documentation.

    Who owns the next step?

    List who makes, imports and distributes the product in each market. Check your role in Chapter IV and producer registration in Article 44. Give each open question an owner.

    Questions about PPWR

    What is PPWR?

    PPWR stands for Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation. Regulation (EU) 2025/40 sets rules for packaging placed on the EU market and covers topics such as packaging design, recyclability and producer responsibility.

    Read Regulation (EU) 2025/40

    When do the new packaging rules apply?

    The PPWR generally applies from 12 August 2026. Some provisions and specific requirements apply on other dates. Check the dates for the requirement and packaging in question.

    Read the European Commission implementation guidance

    What is the difference between PPWR and Danish producer responsibility?

    PPWR is the EU regulation on packaging and packaging waste. Danish producer responsibility is the system under which responsible businesses contribute to the management of packaging waste in Denmark. Check both the requirements for your packaging and your obligations as a producer.

    Read about Danish producer responsibility

    Answer five questions about your business and packaging. You get a prioritised checklist with practical steps for roles, sales markets, packaging materials and documentation.
