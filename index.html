Before you make decisions

Start with evidence.
Then choose your next move.

PPWR affects packaging and the organisations involved in placing it on the market. The details depend on your role, packaging and markets. Use this sequence to get the right people involved.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Map your packaging chain

List products, packaging parts, suppliers, sales markets and the roles involved in buying, filling, importing or distributing each item.

02 · DOCUMENT

Request the missing data

Ask suppliers for component-level material, weight, construction and existing supporting documentation. Record unknowns rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

03 · CLARIFY

Use the right expertise

Check legal interpretation with official sources or qualified advisers. Bring design and prototype questions to a packaging specialist.
